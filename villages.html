Nindo Shaher and Its Communities

Nindo Shaher is part of District Badin in Sindh, Pakistan. Its town and surrounding villages are home to communities with their own local experiences, traditions and ways of life.

This page brings together a directory of villages and an introduction to the local communities. Additional details, photographs and verified information can be included as they become available.
