OUR TOWN, OUR IDENTITY

A Closer Look at Nindo Shaher

Nindo Shaher is a town in District Badin, Sindh, Pakistan. Its identity is connected with the people who live here, their traditions, local communities and surrounding rural life.

This independent information website aims to bring together useful information about the town, its heritage, villages, agriculture, education, healthcare, public places and community activities.
